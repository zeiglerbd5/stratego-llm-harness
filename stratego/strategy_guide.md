# Strategy Guide

- Put the Flag on your back row, in a corner or against an edge, so it has as
  few approach squares as possible. Put Bombs on those approaches.
- Your front row is the contact line. Anything there can be attacked on the
  enemy's first moves. Do not put the Flag, the Spy, or your only Miner there.
- The Spy beats only the Marshal, and only when the Spy attacks. Keep it hidden
  near your own Marshal or General and wait for the enemy Marshal to reveal itself.
- The Miner is the only piece that can remove a Bomb. Keep at least one alive
  and out of early fights; you need it to reach a bombed-in Flag.
- Scouts are for information. Use them to probe pieces that have never moved.
- An enemy piece that has never moved by the middle of the game is probably a
  Bomb or the Flag. Bombs are usually next to the Flag.
- If the game reaches the move cap it is decided on material. When ahead,
  trade pieces and avoid risk. When behind, you must take risks to catch up.
